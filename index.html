Our story

Oak & Honey began in 2019 with one espresso machine, one oven and a lot of borrowed chairs. Two friends wanted a place where students could stay all afternoon and neighbours could catch up without watching the clock.

Today we bake every morning, roast with a local partner and change the menu with the seasons. The room is still full of borrowed chairs. We kept them on purpose.
